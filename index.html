About Me

I got into tech mostly by accident. I took a data entry job to support my community theater hobby and somehow ended up working in an IT department with no prior IT experience. The learning curve was steep, but I found that I really enjoyed troubleshooting, learning how things worked, and solving problems.

A few developers I worked with encouraged me to pursue coding, so I went back to school and made the jump into software development. Now I work as a full-stack developer at DSHS, building and improving applications people rely on every day.

Outside of work, I enjoy running, hiking, community theater, and spending time with my wife.
